# Plazia Links brand assets

Source: `plazia-company`, brand book edition 1.3 (October 2, 2026),
`documents/brand/brand-guidelines.md` and `context/subbranding.yaml`.

`links-color.svg` extends the company product family with the Links name. It was
generated with the existing `tools/render_subbranding.py` in a temporary copy of
the company sources, with `products: [Links]`. Corporate paths, palette D, export
frame, right alignment, 22-unit capital height, tracking and baseline are unchanged.
The descriptor is outlined Manrope SemiBold. Links is an application of the family
rule; it is not an additional product approval recorded in the company manifest.

`p.svg` and `InterVariable.ttf` are unchanged copies of the current company assets.
Interface text uses Inter 400 and 600; the outlined logo needs no runtime Manrope
font. Both fonts' SIL Open Font Licenses are included.
